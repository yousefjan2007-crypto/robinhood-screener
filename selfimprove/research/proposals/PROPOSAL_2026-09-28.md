## 3. Two hypotheses, pre-declared, NOT registered

Both are stated here so that if they are ever written as modules the threshold was on the record
first. Neither is written as code this week, and both carry an explicit registration precondition.

### H1 (entry) — the hour-1 volume floor may be doing all of its work in its upper range

**Hypothesis.** `band_volume_early`'s clauses are right in kind and wrong in degree: at
`vol_h1 ≥ $50k` the champion fires on **521 of 1,526** matured rows (34 %), roughly 3.4× the
~10 % share a champion band was designed to select, and its bound sits at zero. If hour-1 volume is
the real discriminator, a strictly nested band with the floor raised by a factor of ~5 (all other
clauses byte-identical: `pair_age_min ≤ 30`, `liq_usd ≥ 10k`, `buys_h1 ≥ 2 × sells_h1`,
`mcap ≤ 2M`) should raise the day-clustered bound while cutting n by a similar factor.

**Mechanism.** The clone swarms sit *just above* the floor — 6–13 same-name tokens at ~$30k
liquidity and $25–38k **lifetime** volume clear `LIQ_FLOOR_USD` and `MIN_VOL_H24_USD` — while the
reference runners did **$0.97M–$5.13M in hour one**, 20–100× the floor. A floor that both
populations clear separates nothing; the separation, if it exists, lives an order of magnitude up.

**Kill condition.** (a) the raised-floor band's paired lower bound against the champion is ≤ 0 on
its forward prefix, or (b) it cannot reach `BAND_MIN_SELECTED = 150` rows across
`BAND_FWD_MIN_DAYS = 40` days — i.e. raising the floor makes it inert, which is itself the answer
(the floor's upper range is unreachable at this chain's volumes), or (c) coverage below
`BAND_MIN_COVERAGE = 0.80`. Any of the three retires it; K5 retires the family with it.

**Dead ends it does not repeat.** Not item 8 (relaxing the strict band — this is a *tightening*, and
the one direction 14 of 14 Solana relaxations left untested). Not item 7 (the anti-predictive soft
score — the soft-score floor is untouched). Not item 4 (delayed entry — the age clause stays
≤ 30 min, so entry timing is unchanged). Not items 1/2/5/6 (no wallet cohorts, no entity linking,
no snipe features). Not item 12 (no classifier).

**Leakage warning, stated in advance.** The $0.97M–$5.13M figure comes from tokens chosen *because*
they ran (n = 3–5, `docs/RETRO_2026-09-16_hype_runners.md` is explicit that no rule is derived from
it). A floor calibrated against an outcome-selected fixture can only ever be judged forward-only.

**Registration precondition.** ≥ 12 matured alert-days in the entry scorecard, a non-VOID entry-gate
run, **and** a decision on `band_hype_early` (§4) — which already changes the flow clause of the same
band and would confound a volume-floor reading if both entered the pool in the same week.

### H2 (exit) — a sub-15-minute time stop, proposed as a *family-closing negative result*

**Hypothesis.** If the exit ranking is monotone in time-to-exit down to the cost floor, then a
5-minute time stop lands between `sell_15m` (own LB −0.250) and `ctl_exit_immediately` (−0.231),
and **no real policy will ever clear the gate**, because the only thing separating the best real
policy from the do-nothing control is holding time and the limit of that sequence is not trading.

**Mechanism.** Median entry lag is 139 s; the population's decay is already visible inside the first
minutes (§2.2). A time stop pays exactly one modelled round trip (≈ 3.4 % all-in at $10 positions,
of which the fixed gas term alone is ~1.4 %) and holds risk for a bounded window.

**Kill condition (and the point of the trial).** `sell_5m`'s own day-clustered LB ≤
`ctl_exit_immediately`'s own LB at 40 alert-days ⇒ **the exit family is closed** and the honest
statement in `README.md` becomes measured rather than inherited: this system is an alert tool with
no tradable exit. A *win* would be the surprise, and would have to survive forward-only nomination.

**Dead ends it does not repeat.** Not item 3 (that item is *stop-loss* families; this carries no
stop, no trail and no ladder — one key, `max_hold_s`). Not item 4 (delayed *entry*, not exit).

**Registration precondition — and an argument against it.** This costs one permanent trial:
`policies_ever_scored` 16 → 17, which raises the exit DSR bar for every later policy for ever. The
measured bar is already "a per-day Sharpe of about 0.57 at 40 alert-days and 16 cumulative trials",
and no measured policy is within 5× of it. Two policy modules are **already written and unregistered**
(§4), and registering them takes the family to 18. My recommendation is therefore: **do not
register H2 until those two have a verdict.** If the operator wants the family-closing result
sooner, the cheaper instrument is the existing table — `sell_15m` has been the best challenger in
three consecutive weekly runs and has failed the same four checks each time.
